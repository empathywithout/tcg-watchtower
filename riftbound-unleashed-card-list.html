Unleashed

Complete Riftbound Unleashed card list with live market prices. All UNL cards including the Ultimate rarity Baron Nashor and every chase card ranked by value.

Unleashed is the third Riftbound set, released May 8, 2026. It introduces the Ambush, Level, and Hunt keywords alongside the Ultimate rarity — the rarest card type in Riftbound, featuring Baron Nashor as the first Ultimate card, appearing in fewer than 1 in 1,000 packs.

Unleashed FAQ

What is new in Riftbound Unleashed?

Unleashed introduces three new keywords: Ambush (deploy directly to battlefields), Level (spend XP to upgrade cards), and Hunt (gather XP resources). It also introduces the Ultimate rarity, rarer than Signature, with Baron Nashor as the first Ultimate card appearing in fewer than 1 in 1,000 packs.

See all Unleashed chase cards ranked by price →

How many cards are in Riftbound Unleashed?

Unleashed contains 219 total cards, making it the smallest of the first three Riftbound sets.

When did Riftbound Unleashed release?

Unleashed released on May 8, 2026 in English.
