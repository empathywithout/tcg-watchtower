Frequently Asked Questions

What are the chase cards in Origins?

The chase cards in Origins are its highest-rarity pulls: Signature, Overnumbered, Epic. These are the cards collectors specifically hope to pull from a booster box.

What is the most valuable Origins card?

Nine-tailed Fox is currently the most valuable card in Origins as a Signature card, with a normal price of N/A. See all chase cards ranked by price below.

How many chase cards are in Origins?

96 out of 352 total cards in Origins qualify as chase-tier rarities (Signature, Overnumbered, Showcase, Legendary, or Epic).

What is the difference between Normal and Foil in Riftbound?

In Riftbound TCG, most cards come in both a Normal (non-foil) version and a Foil version. Foil cards have a premium finish and are worth more than their normal counterparts. Signature cards are foil-only and represent the rarest treatment in the game.
