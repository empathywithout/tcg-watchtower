Frequently Asked Questions

What are the chase cards in Unleashed?

The chase cards in Unleashed are its highest-rarity pulls: Overnumbered, Signature, Epic. These are the cards collectors specifically hope to pull from a booster box.

What is the most valuable Unleashed card?

Baron Nashor is currently the most valuable card in Unleashed as a Overnumbered card, priced at $1551.34. See all chase cards ranked by price below.

How many chase cards are in Unleashed?

97 out of 286 total cards in Unleashed qualify as chase-tier rarities (Signature, Overnumbered, Showcase, Legendary, or Epic).

What is the difference between Normal and Foil in Riftbound?

In Riftbound TCG, most cards come in both a Normal (non-foil) version and a Foil version. Foil cards have a premium finish and are worth more than their normal counterparts. Signature cards are foil-only and represent the rarest treatment in the game.
