Frequently Asked Questions

What are the chase cards in Spiritforged?

The chase cards in Spiritforged are its highest-rarity pulls: Signature, Overnumbered, Epic. These are the cards collectors specifically hope to pull from a booster box.

What is the most valuable Spiritforged card?

Ahri, Inquisitive is currently the most valuable card in Spiritforged as a Signature card, priced at $2752.05. See all chase cards ranked by price below.

How many chase cards are in Spiritforged?

104 out of 288 total cards in Spiritforged qualify as chase-tier rarities (Signature, Overnumbered, Showcase, Legendary, or Epic).

What is the difference between Normal and Foil in Riftbound?

In Riftbound TCG, most cards come in both a Normal (non-foil) version and a Foil version. Foil cards have a premium finish and are worth more than their normal counterparts. Signature cards are foil-only and represent the rarest treatment in the game.
